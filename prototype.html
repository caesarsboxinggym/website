What we do

Six classes, all ages from six up

Class names and age bands are the club’s own. The descriptions underneath are my draft — rewrite them however you like. draft copy

Kids Boxing

Ages 6 to 12. Footwork, technique and fitness in a group, no contact. Tuesday and Thursday, straight after school.

Teenagers

Ages 13 to 16, training alongside the fighters. Proper boxing, coached carefully, guardian consent on registration.

Fighters Boxing

For anyone competing or working towards it. Pads, sparring and conditioning three nights a week.

Adults Boxing

The late session, open to any ability. Come for the boxing or come for the fitness — nobody has to spar.

Circuit Training

Wednesday conditioning. Stations, timed rounds, no gloves needed. Good on its own or alongside the boxing.

Ladies Bootcamp

Women only, Wednesday evenings. Boxing-based fitness with no sparring and no experience needed.

Pricing

Teenagers & adults £5per session

Or £10 for the whole week — every class in the week you buy it, including nights earlier that week you haven’t paid for yet.

Ladies £5per session

The Wednesday bootcamp or any other session — the same rate.

How the week pass works. It covers the calendar week you buy it in, Monday to Sunday — not the next seven days. Buy one on Thursday and it still settles Monday and Tuesday. Cash or card, both taken at the gym; card goes through the club’s SumUp reader. Monthly at £40 is available if you ask a coach.

Registration

One form, once

  • Name, phone and address, so a coach can find you on the system.
  • Email is optional. Leave it out and we’ll chase any unpaid sessions in person, as now.
  • Next of kin and an emergency contact number.
  • Anything a coach should know — injuries, asthma, medication.
  • Age, so the system knows the right class and the right price.
  • Under 18s — which is most of the Kids and Teenagers classes: guardian name, guardian contact and consent, recorded with the date it was given.
